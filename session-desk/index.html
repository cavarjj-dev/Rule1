Setup playbook

Every rule is read before the open and stated relative to the 4H bias, so a bearish day with the London high swept and a bullish day with the London low swept are the same setup. Hit rates are from the hourly year; the 15M check, target distance and heat are from the 15M sample with the 9:30 open.

How these were found: about 2,500 combinations of pre-open conditions and targets were tested. A rule is listed only if it beats the baseline by 10 points, holds in both halves of the year, and agrees with the 15M sample. Testing that many combinations flatters the winners, so expect live hit rates a few points lower and keep tagging trades by setup.

    Staying on plan

    Words on this page

    Floor
    If your balance touches it, the account is gone. It starts at $48,000 and rises with your best balance (open profit counts) until it stops at $50,000.
    Cushion
    Balance minus floor. The room you have to be wrong.
    Lock
    When your balance peaks at $52,000, the floor stops at $50,000 for good.
    Buffer
    You can only withdraw money above $52,000. You keep 80% of what you withdraw.
    R
    One unit of risk: what you lose if the stop is hit. At 2 MNQ and a 40-pt stop, 1R = $160.
